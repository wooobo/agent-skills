# 렌즈 & 원근 & 피사계심도 (Lens, Perspective, Depth of Field)

같은 피사체·앵글이라도 렌즈 화각과 초점이 공간감을 통째로 바꾼다 — 와이드의 확장, 망원의 압축, 얕은 심도의 분리, 딥 포커스의 또렷함, 광학 특수 효과.

> 각 항목 구성: **시그널**(구도 시그니처) / **앵커**(룩을 고정하는 키워드, 있으면) / **의도**(무드 단어) / **컴포지션 블록**(영어, 바로 붙여쓰기) / **영상 추가**(영상 생성 시 카메라 무브) / **주의**.
>
> 컴포지션 블록은 이미지·영상 **공용**이며 프롬프트 **맨 앞**에 둔다. 초점거리 숫자만 던지지 말고 항상 *효과*(compression, distortion, bokeh)를 함께 묘사한다. 사용법은 `SKILL.md` 참고.



## 렌즈 화각 / 원근

### 와이드앵글 확장 / Wide-Angle Expansion

- **시그널**: 넓은 화각으로 공간을 확장하고 전경을 과장해 몰입감과 스케일을 키우는 룩.
- **앵커**: `wide-angle lens, expansive perspective` · **의도**: expansive, immersive
- **컴포지션 블록**:
  ```
  Wide-angle composition with an expansive field of view that stretches the space, exaggerating the size of near foreground elements while pushing the background far away; strong sense of depth and immersion, lines diverging toward the edges; the subject set within a sweeping environment, deep focus front to back; overall framing — Wide-Angle Expansion, expansive and immersive.
  ```
- **영상 추가**: `bold forward dolly amplifying the exaggerated perspective`
- **주의**: 인물 얼굴이 가까우면 왜곡으로 코·이마가 커짐 — 클로즈업엔 부적합; 가장자리 인물은 늘어남.

### 울트라와이드 왜곡 / Ultra-Wide Distortion

- **시그널**: 극단적 광각이 직선을 휘고 원근을 과장해 역동적이고 비현실적인 공간을 만드는 룩.
- **앵커**: `ultra-wide 14mm, exaggerated distortion` · **의도**: dramatic, surreal
- **컴포지션 블록**:
  ```
  Ultra-wide composition with an extreme field of view that bends straight lines and dramatically exaggerates perspective, near objects ballooning huge while the far world races away tiny; dynamic distorted space full of energy, verticals leaning at the edges; subject thrust close for impact, vast deep focus; overall framing — Ultra-Wide Distortion, dramatic and surreal.
  ```
- **영상 추가**: `aggressive low forward push exaggerating the warp`
- **주의**: 의도적 왜곡 — 자연스러운 인물·제품엔 금물; 수평선 위치에 따라 휘어짐을 통제.

### 피시아이 / Fisheye

- **시그널**: 화면 전체가 구체처럼 둥글게 휘는 극단적 배럴 왜곡의 장난스럽고 강렬한 룩.
- **앵커**: `fisheye lens, barrel distortion` · **의도**: playful, intense
- **컴포지션 블록**:
  ```
  Fisheye composition with extreme barrel distortion curving the entire frame into a bulging sphere, straight lines bowing outward and the center pushed close and large while edges wrap away; hyper-immersive, slightly absurd warped space; subject dead-center where distortion is least, everything else curling around; overall framing — Fisheye, playful and intense.
  ```
- **영상 추가**: `close push-in heightening the spherical bulge`
- **주의**: 강한 스타일 — 진지·우아한 무드와 충돌; 피사체를 중앙에 둬야 덜 왜곡됨.

### 표준 50mm 자연원근 / Standard 50mm Natural

- **시그널**: 사람 눈에 가장 가까운 자연스러운 원근 — 압축도 확장도 없는 정직한 룩.
- **앵커**: `50mm standard lens, natural perspective` · **의도**: natural, honest
- **컴포지션 블록**:
  ```
  Standard-lens composition with a natural fifty-millimeter perspective that closely matches human vision, neither compressing nor stretching space so proportions feel honest and unforced; subject on a third at a comfortable distance, background sitting naturally behind with moderate depth; overall framing — Standard Natural, natural and honest.
  ```
- **영상 추가**: `unobtrusive dolly or steady hold, natural perspective`
- **주의**: 가장 중립적 — 특별한 인상이 필요하면 와이드/망원으로; 평범해 보이지 않게 배치로 흥미를.

### 85mm 포트레이트 압축 / 85mm Portrait Compression

- **시그널**: 살짝 망원으로 얼굴 비례를 아름답게 압축하고 배경을 부드럽게 녹이는 인물 표준 룩.
- **앵커**: `85mm portrait lens, flattering compression` · **의도**: flattering, intimate
- **컴포지션 블록**:
  ```
  Short-telephoto portrait composition with eighty-five-millimeter compression that flatters facial proportions, gently flattening features and rendering the background into smooth creamy blur; subject isolated on a third with soft separation, the world melting behind; shallow depth of field, beautiful bokeh; overall framing — 85mm Portrait, flattering and intimate.
  ```
- **영상 추가**: `slow push-in, background bokeh drifting`
- **주의**: 인물 클로즈업의 표준 — 와이드 왜곡을 피하려면 이걸; 배경과의 거리가 있어야 분리가 산다.

### 망원 200mm 압축 / Telephoto Compression

- **시그널**: 긴 망원이 원근을 납작하게 눌러 멀리 있는 피사체와 배경을 한 평면처럼 겹치는 룩.
- **앵커**: `telephoto 200mm, compressed perspective` · **의도**: compressed, cinematic
- **컴포지션 블록**:
  ```
  Telephoto composition with strong perspective compression that flattens depth, pulling the distant background forward so it stacks close behind the subject in layered planes; the subject isolated against the compressed, softly blurred backdrop on a third; shallow depth, layers pressed together; overall framing — Telephoto Compression, compressed and cinematic.
  ```
- **영상 추가**: `slow telephoto track, compressed background creeping`
- **주의**: 피사체와 배경이 떨어져 있을 때 압축이 극적; 손떨림에 민감(영상은 안정적 무브로).

### 슈퍼텔레 / 거대 태양·달 / Super-Telephoto

- **시그널**: 초망원이 배경의 태양·달·도시를 거대하게 끌어와 피사체 뒤에 붙이는 압도적 압축 룩.
- **앵커**: `super-telephoto, giant sun/moon backdrop` · **의도**: epic, surreal
- **컴포지션 블록**:
  ```
  Super-telephoto composition with extreme compression that magnifies a distant sun, moon or skyline into a giant glowing backdrop looming huge directly behind the small silhouetted subject; depth crushed to almost flat, the colossal background dominating the frame; subject low on a third against the swollen disc; overall framing — Super-Telephoto, epic and surreal.
  ```
- **영상 추가**: `very slow long-lens track, the giant backdrop barely shifting`
- **주의**: 배경 천체가 비현실적으로 크지만 의도된 효과 — 자연스러움을 원하면 줄일 것; 매우 안정적 무브 필요.

### 아나모픽 와이드스크린 / Anamorphic Widescreen

- **시그널**: 가로로 긴 2.39:1 시네마 프레임에 타원형 보케와 수평 블루 플레어가 깔리는 영화적 룩.
- **앵커**: `anamorphic widescreen 2.39:1, oval bokeh` · **의도**: cinematic, epic
- **컴포지션 블록**:
  ```
  Anamorphic widescreen composition in a wide 2.39:1 frame with the elongated horizontal field, oval-shaped bokeh and characteristic streaking horizontal lens flares; subject placed on a third within the broad cinematic letterbox, generous horizontal negative space; shallow depth with stretched oval out-of-focus highlights; overall framing — Anamorphic Widescreen, cinematic and epic.
  ```
- **영상 추가**: `slow lateral track exploiting the wide frame, horizontal flare drift`
- **주의**: 21:9 비율로 생성해야 효과 — 9:16 세로엔 부적합; 플레어 과용은 산만.

### 포스드 퍼스펙티브 / Forced Perspective

- **시그널**: 원근 착시로 멀고 가까운 사물의 크기 관계를 조작해 비현실적 상호작용을 만드는 룩.
- **앵커**: `forced perspective optical illusion` · **의도**: playful, illusory
- **컴포지션 블록**:
  ```
  Forced-perspective composition exploiting the optical illusion of distance so a far object appears to interact with a near one at the wrong scale — a tiny figure seeming to hold a distant sun, or pinch a faraway tower; both planes kept in sharp deep focus so the trick reads, subjects aligned across the depth; overall framing — Forced Perspective, playful and illusory.
  ```
- **영상 추가**: `static or very slow move preserving the alignment illusion`
- **주의**: 두 평면이 모두 선명해야(딥 포커스) 착시가 성립; 정렬이 어긋나면 효과가 깨짐.

### 원포인트 선원근 / One-Point Linear Perspective

- **시그널**: 평행선이 한 소실점으로 수렴하는 강한 선원근으로 깊이와 질서를 만드는 룩.
- **앵커**: `one-point linear perspective` · **의도**: deep, ordered
- **컴포지션 블록**:
  ```
  One-point linear-perspective composition with parallel edges receding to a single vanishing point, building strong ordered depth and pulling the eye straight into the frame; the subject anchored near the vanishing point or on a third along the converging lines; crisp deep focus along the corridor of space; overall framing — One-Point Perspective, deep and ordered.
  ```
- **영상 추가**: `straight dolly-in along the perspective lines`
- **주의**: 베니싱 포인트(구성원리)와 렌즈 원근이 함께 작동 — 선이 깔끔히 수렴하게 정렬.



## 피사계심도

### 얕은 심도 크리미 보케 / Shallow DOF · Creamy Bokeh

- **시그널**: 피사체만 칼같이 선명하고 배경은 부드러운 보케로 녹아 강한 분리와 친밀감을 주는 룩.
- **앵커**: `shallow depth of field, creamy bokeh` · **의도**: intimate, dreamy
- **컴포지션 블록**:
  ```
  Shallow-depth-of-field composition with a razor-thin plane of focus on the subject while the background dissolves into soft creamy bokeh, isolating the figure with dreamy separation; subject on a third popping crisply against the melted backdrop, foreground also softening; luminous out-of-focus highlights; overall framing — Shallow DOF Bokeh, intimate and dreamy.
  ```
- **영상 추가**: `slow push-in, bokeh orbs drifting and breathing`
- **주의**: 초점면이 얕아 눈/핵심에 정확히 맞춰야 — 빗나가면 흐릿; 배경과 거리가 있어야 보케가 산다.

### 딥 포커스 전체 선명 / Deep Focus

- **시그널**: 전경부터 배경까지 전부 또렷해 화면 전 영역이 동시에 의미를 갖는 고전적 룩.
- **앵커**: `deep focus, everything sharp` · **의도**: detailed, immersive
- **컴포지션 블록**:
  ```
  Deep-focus composition keeping everything from the immediate foreground to the far background in crisp sharp focus at once, so every plane carries information and the eye roams the whole frame; subjects staged across the depth, each layer readable, foreground and distance equally defined; expansive sharp space; overall framing — Deep Focus, detailed and immersive.
  ```
- **영상 추가**: `slow move with full depth staying sharp, strong parallax`
- **주의**: 여러 층을 의미 있게 배치해야 — 안 그러면 그냥 다 선명한 평범 컷; 얕은 심도 의도와 섞지 말 것.

### 전경 보케 베일 / Foreground Bokeh Veil

- **시그널**: 흐릿한 전경 물체가 베일처럼 깔려 피사체를 들여다보는 듯한 부드러운 깊이를 더하는 룩.
- **앵커**: `out-of-focus foreground veil` · **의도**: dreamy, layered
- **컴포지션 블록**:
  ```
  Foreground-veil composition placing soft out-of-focus elements — flowers, leaves, lights, fabric — across the immediate foreground so a dreamy blurred veil partially drapes over the sharp subject beyond; the gauzy near layer adds intimacy and depth, the subject crisp on a third behind it; strong focus gradient front to subject; overall framing — Foreground Bokeh Veil, dreamy and layered.
  ```
- **영상 추가**: `slow drift letting the foreground veil glide across the frame`
- **주의**: 전경 베일이 피사체 핵심(특히 눈)을 덮지 않게; 충분히 흐려 '얼룩'으로 안 보이게.

### 매크로 극접사 / Macro

- **시그널**: 아주 가까이서 미세 대상을 화면 가득 잡아 질감과 디테일을 극대화하되 심도가 극도로 얕은 룩.
- **앵커**: `macro extreme close focus` · **의도**: tactile, revealing
- **컴포지션 블록**:
  ```
  Macro composition magnifying a tiny subject — a droplet, insect, fiber, eye — to fill the frame with extraordinary texture and detail, the world rendered at life-size or larger; an extremely thin plane of focus on the key surface with everything just millimeters away dissolving into blur; intimate revealing depth; overall framing — Macro, tactile and revealing.
  ```
- **영상 추가**: `micro focus pull crawling across the tiny surface`
- **주의**: 심도가 극히 얕아 초점 위치가 결정적 — 핵심 디테일에 정확히; 살짝만 벗어나도 다 흐림.

### 틸트시프트 미니어처 / Tilt-Shift Miniature

- **시그널**: 선택적 초점 띠로 실제 풍경을 장난감 미니어처처럼 보이게 하는 룩.
- **앵커**: `tilt-shift miniature effect` · **의도**: whimsical, toy-like
- **컴포지션 블록**:
  ```
  Tilt-shift composition with a narrow horizontal band of sharp focus across the middle and strong blur above and below, making a real scene look like a tiny toy model viewed from above; a high angle looking down on the miniature world, saturated playful tones, subjects rendered as little figurines; selective plane focus; overall framing — Tilt-Shift Miniature, whimsical and toy-like.
  ```
- **영상 추가**: `slightly sped-up motion under the static tilt-shift band`
- **주의**: 보통 하이앵글에서 효과 — 아이레벨엔 약함; 초점 띠 위치로 '미니어처'로 읽히게.

### 스플릿 다이옵터 / Split Diopter

- **시그널**: 아주 가까운 것과 아주 먼 것을 동시에 선명하게 잡아 두 거리를 한 프레임에 병치하는 고전 기법.
- **앵커**: `split diopter, near and far both sharp` · **의도**: tense, theatrical
- **컴포지션 블록**:
  ```
  Split-diopter composition holding both a very close foreground element and a distant background subject in sharp focus simultaneously, with the unfocused middle distance between them, juxtaposing two planes of action in one frame; a near face on one third and a far figure on the other, both crisp, charged with tension; theatrical divided depth; overall framing — Split Diopter, tense and theatrical.
  ```
- **영상 추가**: `static hold preserving the dual-focus planes`
- **주의**: 근·원 두 대상을 좌우/대각으로 분리 배치; 중간거리는 흐려도 자연스럽게 구성.

### 랙 포커스 / Rack Focus (영상)

- **시그널**: 초점을 한 피사체에서 다른 피사체로 옮겨 시선과 의미를 전환하는 영상 전용 기법.
- **앵커**: `rack focus pull between subjects` · **의도**: revealing, directed
- **컴포지션 블록**:
  ```
  Rack-focus composition staging two subjects at different distances on a shallow plane so focus can shift from one to the other, redirecting attention and revealing relationships within a single shot; near and far figures each placed on a third, only one sharp at a time against soft separation; shallow depth built for the pull; overall framing — Rack Focus, revealing and directed.
  ```
- **영상 추가**: `deliberate focus pull from the foreground subject to the background subject`
- **주의**: 영상 전용 — 정지 이미지에선 한쪽만 선명하게 표현; 두 피사체 거리차가 있어야 풀이 보임.

### 환경 인물 / Environmental Portrait

- **시그널**: 인물을 넓은 맥락 속에 작게 두되 또렷하게 잡아 인물과 그의 세계를 함께 말하는 룩.
- **앵커**: `wide environmental portrait` · **의도**: contextual, storytelling
- **컴포지션 블록**:
  ```
  Environmental-portrait composition placing the subject small within a wide, characterful setting that tells their story, yet kept sharp and clearly the focal point through placement and light; figure on a third surrounded by meaningful context, deep focus tying person to place; rich layered depth; overall framing — Environmental Portrait, contextual and storytelling.
  ```
- **영상 추가**: `slow pull-back revealing more of the subject's world`
- **주의**: 맥락이 인물을 삼키지 않게 빛·톤·배치로 인물을 분리; 배경 요소가 의미를 가져야.



## 광학 특수 프레이밍

### 실루엣 콘트르주르 / Silhouette · Contre-jour

- **시그널**: 강한 역광으로 피사체를 검은 실루엣으로 만들어 윤곽과 형태만 남기는 그래픽한 룩.
- **앵커**: `silhouette, backlit contre-jour` · **의도**: graphic, dramatic
- **컴포지션 블록**:
  ```
  Silhouette composition with strong backlight rendering the subject as a clean dark shape against a bright glowing background, reducing the figure to its outline and gesture; the silhouette placed on a third against the luminous sky or window, form reading purely through contour; flat high-contrast depth; overall framing — Silhouette Contre-jour, graphic and dramatic.
  ```
- **영상 추가**: `slow move as the bright backdrop shifts behind the dark shape`
- **주의**: 실루엣이 읽히는 명확한 포즈/윤곽이어야 — 겹치면 덩어리로 뭉개짐; 배경이 충분히 밝아야.

### 렌즈 플레어 프레이밍 / Lens Flare Framing

- **시그널**: 프레임 안에 광원을 넣어 플레어·고스트가 번지며 분위기와 빛의 존재감을 더하는 룩.
- **앵커**: `lens flare in frame, sun streaks` · **의도**: warm, atmospheric
- **컴포지션 블록**:
  ```
  Lens-flare composition with a bright light source just inside the frame throwing streaks, ghosts and a hazy bloom across the image, adding warmth and a tangible sense of light; the subject placed on a third where the flare grazes rather than buries it, glow washing one side; soft veiled depth; overall framing — Lens Flare Framing, warm and atmospheric.
  ```
- **영상 추가**: `slow move sweeping the light source so the flare drifts and pulses`
- **주의**: 플레어가 피사체·핵심을 덮지 않게 가장자리로; 과하면 디테일이 씻겨 산만.

### 리플렉션 / 미러 더블 / Reflection · Mirror Double

- **시그널**: 거울·물·유리의 반사로 피사체를 이중으로 잡아 대칭·이중성을 만드는 룩.
- **앵커**: `reflection mirror double` · **의도**: doubled, poetic
- **컴포지션 블록**:
  ```
  Reflection composition pairing the subject with its mirrored image in glass, water or a polished surface, doubling the figure for symmetry and a poetic sense of duality; the real subject and its reflection balanced across the reflective edge, each on its own third; layered depth between surface and image; overall framing — Mirror Double, doubled and poetic.
  ```
- **영상 추가**: `slow move where real and reflection shift in counterpoint`
- **주의**: 반사면이 깨끗·뚜렷해야; 실물과 반사의 관계(대칭·왜곡)를 의도적으로 구성.

### 굴절 / 유리 너머 / Refraction · Through-Glass

- **시그널**: 크리스털 볼·물방울·비 젖은 유리 너머로 굴절된 상을 잡아 몽환적 왜곡을 더하는 룩.
- **앵커**: `refraction through glass, crystal ball` · **의도**: dreamy, distorted
- **컴포지션 블록**:
