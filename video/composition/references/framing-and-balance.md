# 프레이밍 구조 & 균형 & 깊이 (Framing, Balance, Depth)

피사체를 화면 어디에 어떻게 배치하고, 시선을 어떻게 유도하며, 깊이를 어떻게 쌓는지 — 구도의 "구성 원리".

> 각 항목 구성: **시그널**(구도 시그니처) / **앵커**(룩을 고정하는 키워드, 있으면) / **의도**(무드 단어) / **컴포지션 블록**(영어, 바로 붙여쓰기) / **영상 추가**(영상 생성 시 카메라 무브) / **주의**.
>
> 컴포지션 블록은 이미지·영상 **공용**이며 프롬프트 **맨 앞**에 둔다. 영상은 블록을 그대로 쓰고 *영상 추가* 토큰을 덧붙인다. **구성 원리는 한 컷에 하나만 지배적으로** — 두 개 이상 겹치면 상쇄된다. 사용법은 `SKILL.md` 참고.



## 격자 / 대칭 구성

### 삼분할 / Rule of Thirds

- **시그널**: 화면을 3×3 격자로 나눠 피사체를 교차점·세로선에 놓는 가장 기본적이고 자연스러운 배치.
- **앵커**: `rule of thirds` · **의도**: balanced, natural
- **컴포지션 블록**:
  ```
  Rule-of-thirds composition with the subject placed on a third line rather than the center, its key point of interest sitting on one of the four grid intersections; the gaze or motion leads into the open two-thirds of negative space, the horizon aligned to the upper or lower third; balanced, easy depth; overall framing — Rule of Thirds, balanced and natural.
  ```
- **영상 추가**: `slow drift keeping the subject anchored on the third`
- **주의**: 중앙 대칭 의도와 섞지 말 것; 눈/초점이 교차점에 오게, 시선 방향에 여백을.

### 중앙 데드센터 / 원포인트 / Dead-Center · One-Point

- **시그널**: 피사체를 정확히 한가운데 박아 대칭 배경과 함께 강박적 질서를 만드는 큐브릭/웨스 앤더슨식 컷.
- **앵커**: `dead-center one-point composition` · **의도**: ordered, hypnotic
- **컴포지션 블록**:
  ```
  Dead-center composition with the subject planted exactly in the middle of the frame, flanked by symmetrical surroundings that converge toward a single central vanishing point; perfectly level horizon, mirrored balance on both sides, an obsessive ordered stillness; deep one-point perspective drawing the eye straight in; overall framing — Dead-Center One-Point, ordered and hypnotic.
  ```
- **영상 추가**: `dead-straight slow dolly-in along the central axis`
- **주의**: 배경까지 대칭이어야 효과가 산다 — 어긋난 배경은 인상을 깬다; 평범한 장면에 쓰면 그냥 가운데 배치로 보임.

### 좌우 대칭 미러 / Symmetrical Mirror

- **시그널**: 중심축을 기준으로 좌우(또는 상하)가 거울처럼 반사되는 정적·격식 있는 구도.
- **앵커**: `symmetrical mirror composition` · **의도**: formal, serene
- **컴포지션 블록**:
  ```
  Symmetrical composition mirrored across a central axis so the left and right halves echo each other in shape and weight; subject straddling or centered on the axis, reflective balance creating calm formal order; even depth with the symmetry reinforced by architecture or repeating elements; overall framing — Symmetrical Mirror, formal and serene.
  ```
- **영상 추가**: `level push-in or pull-out preserving the symmetry`
- **주의**: 완벽 대칭은 경직될 수 있으니 작은 비대칭 포인트(인물·색)로 생기를; 수평·수직을 정확히.

### 황금비 / 피보나치 스파이럴 / Golden Ratio · Fibonacci Spiral

- **시그널**: 황금나선을 따라 요소를 배치해 시선이 부드럽게 초점으로 감기는 유기적 구도.
- **앵커**: `golden ratio Fibonacci spiral` · **의도**: organic, harmonious
- **컴포지션 블록**:
  ```
  Golden-ratio composition arranging elements along a Fibonacci spiral so the eye curls naturally inward to the focal point nested in the tight of the spiral; supporting shapes and lines flow along the curve, weight distributed by the phi proportion rather than a rigid grid; gentle layered depth; overall framing — Golden Spiral, organic and harmonious.
  ```
- **영상 추가**: `slow curving move that follows the spiral toward the focal point`
- **주의**: 삼분할과 비슷하나 더 유기적 — 둘을 동시에 강조하지 말 것; 초점을 나선 중심에 둘 것.

### 골든 트라이앵글 / Golden Triangles

- **시그널**: 대각선과 그 수선으로 화면을 삼각 구획해 동적 대각 균형을 만드는 구도.
- **앵커**: `golden triangle composition` · **의도**: dynamic, balanced
- **컴포지션 블록**:
  ```
  Golden-triangle composition dividing the frame with a dominant diagonal and perpendiculars dropped to the opposite corners, placing the subject and counter-elements within the resulting triangles; dynamic diagonal balance with tension held across the slope; layered depth along the diagonal; overall framing — Golden Triangles, dynamic and balanced.
  ```
- **영상 추가**: `move tracking along the dominant diagonal`
- **주의**: 대각이 자연스러운 장면(계단·시선·길)에서 강함; 억지 대각은 어색.

### 균형 / Balanced

- **시그널**: 주 피사체의 무게를 반대편 보조 요소가 받쳐 화면 전체가 안정되게 느껴지는 구도.
- **앵커**: `balanced composition` · **의도**: stable, resolved
- **컴포지션 블록**:
  ```
  Balanced composition where the visual weight of the main subject on one side is answered by a smaller counter-element on the other, so the frame feels evenly resolved and at rest; subject on a third with the counterweight opposite, calm distribution of mass; settled moderate depth; overall framing — Balanced, stable and resolved.
  ```
- **영상 추가**: `steady locked or gentle symmetric move preserving balance`
- **주의**: 카운터웨이트가 주 피사체를 빼앗을 만큼 크면 안 됨 — 보조는 보조답게.

### 비대칭 균형 / 텐션 / Asymmetrical Balance · Tension

- **시그널**: 무게를 일부러 한쪽에 몰아 불안정한 긴장과 동세를 주는 구도.
- **앵커**: `asymmetrical tension composition` · **의도**: tense, dynamic
- **컴포지션 블록**:
  ```
  Asymmetrical composition loading most of the visual weight to one side and leaving the other deliberately open, creating unresolved tension and a sense of imminent movement; subject pushed hard toward one edge with a large void opposite; uneven depth heightening the imbalance; overall framing — Asymmetrical Tension, tense and dynamic.
  ```
- **영상 추가**: `drift that lets the imbalance breathe, or settle into it`
- **주의**: 의도된 불균형임이 읽히게 — 어중간하면 그냥 잘못 배치한 컷; 빈 쪽을 충분히 비울 것.

### 엣지 푸시 / Edge-Push

- **시그널**: 피사체를 프레임 극단 가장자리로 밀어 불안·고립·궁금증을 만드는 과감한 배치.
- **앵커**: `subject pushed to the frame edge` · **의도**: edgy, isolating
- **컴포지션 블록**:
  ```
  Edge-push composition forcing the subject hard against the extreme edge of the frame, even partially cropped, leaving a vast dominant void filling the rest; the off-balance placement creates unease and draws curiosity to what lies beyond the edge; flat or layered depth behind the void; overall framing — Edge-Push, edgy and isolating.
  ```
- **영상 추가**: `slow pan revealing the void, or push that re-centers the subject`
- **주의**: 잘림이 실수처럼 보이지 않게 단호하게; 비운 공간이 의미 있어야 함.



## 선 / 형태 유도

### 리딩 라인 / Leading Lines

- **시그널**: 길·난간·시선 등 선이 시선을 피사체로 끌고 가는 구도.
- **앵커**: `leading lines` · **의도**: directional, immersive
- **컴포지션 블록**:
  ```
  Leading-lines composition using roads, rails, edges or light to draw strong converging lines from the frame edges inward to the subject, guiding the eye straight to the focal point; the subject sits where the lines meet, on a third or at the vanishing point; deep perspective along the lines; overall framing — Leading Lines, directional and immersive.
  ```
- **영상 추가**: `dolly forward along the leading lines toward the subject`
- **주의**: 선이 피사체를 지나쳐 프레임 밖으로 시선을 빼지 않게 — 선의 끝에 피사체를.

### 다이애고널 / 대각 / Diagonal

- **시그널**: 강한 대각선이 화면을 가로질러 에너지와 동세를 만드는 구도.
- **앵커**: `dynamic diagonal composition` · **의도**: energetic, kinetic
- **컴포지션 블록**:
  ```
  Diagonal composition built on a dominant slanting line that cuts across the frame corner to corner, charging the image with movement and energy; subject aligned along or anchored at the end of the diagonal, weight tilting the eye through the frame; dynamic depth following the slope; overall framing — Dynamic Diagonal, energetic and kinetic.
  ```
- **영상 추가**: `track or whip along the diagonal axis`
- **주의**: 수평·수직이 지배적인 장면에 억지 대각은 어색; 더치앵글과 혼동 말 것(이건 피사체·선의 대각, 수평선은 유지).

### 트라이앵글 / 피라미드 / Triangular · Pyramidal

- **시그널**: 요소들이 삼각형·피라미드 형태로 모여 안정감과 위계를 만드는 고전 회화적 구도.
- **앵커**: `triangular pyramidal composition` · **의도**: stable, classical
- **컴포지션 블록**:
  ```
  Triangular composition arranging the subjects into a stable pyramid shape with a broad base and an apex, lending classical balance and a clear hierarchy; the peak element draws the eye while the base grounds the frame, mass settling into the triangle; layered depth within the form; overall framing — Triangular Pyramidal, stable and classical.
  ```
- **영상 추가**: `slow push-in toward the apex of the triangle`
- **주의**: 인물 배치가 부자연스럽게 '연출된 삼각형'이 되지 않게 동기 있는 자세로.

### S-커브 / S-Curve

- **시그널**: 강·길·인체가 S자로 굽이쳐 시선을 우아하게 프레임 깊숙이 끌고 가는 구도.
- **앵커**: `S-curve serpentine composition` · **의도**: graceful, flowing
- **컴포지션 블록**:
  ```
  S-curve composition with a river, path, body or line of light meandering in a serpentine S through the frame, leading the eye gracefully from foreground into the deep distance; the curve organizes the space and carries the focal point along its bend; rich receding depth; overall framing — S-Curve, graceful and flowing.
  ```
- **영상 추가**: `camera glides following the S-curve into depth`
- **주의**: 곡선이 명확히 S로 읽히게 — 어중간한 곡선은 힘이 없다; 곡선 끝/굽이에 초점을.

### 베니싱 포인트 / 원근 수렴 / Vanishing Point

- **시그널**: 모든 선이 한 소실점으로 수렴해 강한 깊이와 끌림을 만드는 원근 구도.
- **앵커**: `one-point perspective vanishing point` · **의도**: deep, hypnotic
- **컴포지션 블록**:
  ```
  Vanishing-point composition where parallel lines — walls, tracks, rows — race inward and converge on a single point deep in the frame, creating powerful pulling perspective; the subject placed at or near the vanishing point as the inevitable destination of the eye; immense receding depth; overall framing — Vanishing Point, deep and hypnotic.
  ```
- **영상 추가**: `straight dolly-in toward the vanishing point`
- **주의**: 소실점이 너무 중앙이면 데드센터와 겹침 — 의도 선택; 수렴선이 깔끔히 정렬돼야.

### 레이디얼 / 방사 / Radial

- **시그널**: 요소들이 한 중심에서 사방으로 방사돼 폭발적 에너지와 집중을 만드는 구도.
- **앵커**: `radial composition` · **의도**: explosive, focal
- **컴포지션 블록**:
  ```
  Radial composition with elements and lines bursting outward from a single central hub, the spokes radiating to the edges so the eye is pulled forcefully to the core; the subject anchors the center of the starburst, surrounded by symmetric radiating energy; flattened graphic depth; overall framing — Radial, explosive and focal.
  ```
- **영상 추가**: `slow rotation around the radial center, or push into the hub`
- **주의**: 중심이 비면 구도가 무너짐 — 허브에 명확한 초점을; 천장·계단·자연 패턴에 강함.

### 패턴 반복 / Repetition · Pattern

- **시그널**: 같은 형태가 규칙적으로 반복돼 리듬과 추상적 질서를 만드는 구도.
- **앵커**: `repetition and pattern` · **의도**: rhythmic, graphic
- **컴포지션 블록**:
  ```
  Repetition composition filling the frame with a regular repeating pattern of identical shapes — windows, columns, tiles, crowds — creating rhythm and graphic order; the eye reads the cadence across the whole field, subject either blending in or anchoring one node; flat even depth emphasizing the pattern; overall framing — Repetition Pattern, rhythmic and graphic.
  ```
- **영상 추가**: `lateral slide across the pattern, or pull-back revealing its scale`
- **주의**: 완전 균질하면 지루할 수 있어 작은 변주/초점이 필요(→ 패턴 브레이크 참고).

### 패턴 브레이크 / 이질점 / Pattern Break

- **시그널**: 반복 패턴 속 단 하나의 이질적 요소가 시선을 빨아들이는 강한 초점 구도.
- **앵커**: `break the pattern, odd one out` · **의도**: focal, witty
- **컴포지션 블록**:
  ```
  Pattern-break composition setting up a uniform repeating field and then disrupting it with a single odd element — different in color, shape or direction — that instantly seizes the eye; the repetition fills the frame as backdrop while the break sits on a third as the unmistakable focal point; flat graphic depth; overall framing — Pattern Break, focal and witty.
  ```
- **영상 추가**: `slow reveal of the odd element within the repeating field`
- **주의**: 이질점이 하나여야 강함 — 둘 이상이면 약해짐; 패턴이 충분히 확립된 뒤 깨야.



## 공간 / 여백

### 네거티브 스페이스 / Negative Space

- **시그널**: 피사체를 작게 두고 넓은 빈 공간으로 둘러싸 고립·고요·스케일을 강조하는 미니멀 구도.
- **앵커**: `negative space, minimalist` · **의도**: isolating, calm
- **컴포지션 블록**:
  ```
  Negative-space composition placing a small subject within a vast expanse of empty, uncluttered space that dominates the frame, isolating the figure and amplifying quiet and scale; subject set on a third or near an edge, the void carrying most of the weight; simple flat depth; overall framing — Negative Space, isolating and calm.
  ```
- **영상 추가**: `slow pull-out enlarging the void around the subject`
- **주의**: 빈 공간이 의미 있고 깨끗해야 — 잡티가 있으면 미니멀이 깨짐; 피사체 톤 대비로 분리.

### 프레임 가득 채우기 / Fill the Frame

- **시그널**: 피사체로 화면을 빈틈없이 꽉 채워 몰입과 질감을 극대화하는 타이트 구도.
- **앵커**: `fill the frame, tight crop` · **의도**: immersive, bold
- **컴포지션 블록**:
  ```
  Fill-the-frame composition cropping in so the subject occupies the entire image edge to edge with no surrounding space, immersing the viewer in its detail and texture; the form bleeds past all four edges, weight evenly saturating the frame; shallow depth on the key surface; overall framing — Fill the Frame, immersive and bold.
  ```
- **영상 추가**: `slow drift across the filled surface, micro push-in`
- **주의**: 너무 채우면 맥락이 사라짐 — 무엇인지 읽히는 선에서; 핵심 부분이 잘려나가지 않게.

### 헤드룸 & 룩룸 / Headroom & Lead Room

- **시그널**: 머리 위 여백(헤드룸)과 시선/이동 방향 앞 여백(룩룸)을 적절히 둬 인물이 편안히 자리잡게 하는 기본 원리.
- **앵커**: `proper headroom and lead room` · **의도**: comfortable, correct
- **컴포지션 블록**:
  ```
  Composition with deliberate headroom above the subject and generous lead room opening in the direction the subject faces or moves, so the figure sits comfortably and has space to look or travel into; subject offset toward the trailing edge on a third, the open room ahead balancing the frame; natural depth; overall framing — Headroom & Lead Room, comfortable and correct.
  ```
- **영상 추가**: `track that preserves lead room ahead of the moving subject`
- **주의**: 시선 뒤가 비고 앞이 막히면 답답하고 잘못돼 보임 — 시선 앞을 비울 것; 헤드룸 과다는 인물을 가라앉힘.

### 로우 호라이즌 / 큰 하늘 / Low Horizon

- **시그널**: 수평선을 화면 아래 1/3 이하로 낮춰 하늘·천장이 압도적으로 펼쳐지는 구도.
- **앵커**: `low horizon, big sky` · **의도**: expansive, airy
- **컴포지션 블록**:
  ```
  Low-horizon composition dropping the horizon line into the bottom third so a vast sky or ceiling fills most of the frame, dwarfing the small subject below; the figure anchored low on a third against the towering openness above; deep airy space with layered clouds or light; overall framing — Low Horizon, expansive and airy.
  ```
- **영상 추가**: `slow tilt up into the sky, or static hold under the openness`
- **주의**: 하늘이 단조로우면 비어 보임 — 구름·빛·그라데이션으로 채울 것; 피사체를 너무 작게 두지 말 것.

### 하이 호라이즌 / 큰 전경 / High Horizon

- **시그널**: 수평선을 위로 올려 지면·전경이 화면 대부분을 채우는 대지 중심 구도.
- **앵커**: `high horizon, dominant foreground` · **의도**: grounded, immersive
- **컴포지션 블록**:
  ```
  High-horizon composition pushing the horizon into the upper third so the ground, water or foreground terrain sweeps across most of the frame; the eye travels over the rich near-to-far surface to the subject and the thin strip of sky above; deep receding ground layers; overall framing — High Horizon, grounded and immersive.
  ```
- **영상 추가**: `low forward dolly skimming over the foreground toward the horizon`
- **주의**: 전경이 비거나 단조로우면 지루 — 질감·리딩 요소를 깔 것.

### 미니멀 고립 / Minimalist Isolation

- **시그널**: 극도로 단순한 배경에 단 하나의 요소만 남겨 정제된 고요를 만드는 구도.
- **앵커**: `minimalist isolation, single element` · **의도**: serene, refined
- **컴포지션 블록**:
  ```
  Minimalist composition reducing the frame to a single clean element against an almost empty, uniform background, stripping away all distraction for refined stillness; the lone subject placed thoughtfully on a third within the calm field; flat simple depth, soft separation; overall framing — Minimalist Isolation, serene and refined.
  ```
- **영상 추가**: `very slow, almost imperceptible push or static hold`
- **주의**: 배경이 정말 깨끗해야 — 잡티 하나가 미니멀을 깬다; 네거티브 스페이스보다 더 절제.

### 탑헤비 / 바텀헤비 / Top-Heavy · Bottom-Heavy

- **시그널**: 시각 무게를 위 또는 아래로 의도적으로 몰아 압박감·안정감을 조절하는 구도.
- **앵커**: `top-heavy / bottom-heavy framing` · **의도**: weighted, expressive
- **컴포지션 블록**:
  ```
  Weighted composition loading the visual mass toward the top of the frame (pressing down, looming) or toward the bottom (settled, anchored), leaving the opposite zone open; the subject and dominant elements cluster in the heavy band while the light band breathes; depth reinforcing the weighted zone; overall framing — Weighted Frame, weighted and expressive.
  ```
- **영상 추가**: `tilt that shifts the weight from one zone to the other`
- **주의**: 어느 쪽이 무거운지 의도를 정하고 일관되게; 어중간하면 그냥 불균형으로 보임.

### 도넛 / 빈 중앙 / Donut · Empty Center

- **시그널**: 중앙을 비우고 요소들을 가장자리에 둘러 배치해 긴장된 공백을 만드는 구도.
- **앵커**: `empty center, donut composition` · **의도**: tense, unconventional
- **컴포지션 블록**:
  ```
  Empty-center composition arranging the elements around the perimeter of the frame while leaving the middle deliberately void, creating a ring of interest around a hollow core that holds an uneasy tension; the eye circles the edges, the central gap charged with absence; even depth around the void; overall framing — Empty Center, tense and unconventional.
  ```
- **영상 추가**: `slow push toward the empty center, or rotation around the ring`
- **주의**: 중앙 공백이 의도임이 읽히게 — 아니면 초점을 놓친 컷처럼 보임; 가장자리 요소들이 시선을 잡아야.



## 깊이 / 레이어

### 전경-중경-배경 3층 / Foreground-Midground-Background

- **시그널**: 명확히 구분된 세 깊이 층을 쌓아 입체적이고 풍부한 공간감을 만드는 구도.
- **앵커**: `foreground-midground-background layers` · **의도**: dimensional, rich
- **컴포지션 블록**:
  ```
  Layered-depth composition with a clear foreground element, a midground subject and a distinct background, each plane separated by scale, light or focus so the eye reads real three-dimensional space; the subject anchors the midground on a third while near and far layers frame it; rich receding depth; overall framing — Layered Depth, dimensional and rich.
  ```
- **영상 추가**: `lateral dolly producing strong parallax between the three layers`
- **주의**: 층이 뭉개지지 않게 톤·초점·스케일로 분리; 전경이 피사체를 가리지 않게.

### 전경 오클루전 / 가림 / Foreground Occlusion

- **시그널**: 잎·창틀·군중 등 전경 물체가 일부를 가려 관음적 깊이와 현장감을 더하는 구도.
- **앵커**: `shooting through foreground objects` · **의도**: voyeuristic, layered
- **컴포지션 블록**:
  ```
  Foreground-occlusion composition shooting past out-of-focus objects in the immediate foreground — leaves, railings, a crowd, glass — that partially obscure the view and frame the sharp subject beyond; the soft near clutter adds depth and a stolen, in-the-moment feel; strong focus separation between the blurred foreground and crisp subject; overall framing — Foreground Occlusion, voyeuristic and layered.
  ```
- **영상 추가**: `slow track letting foreground objects sweep across the lens`
- **주의**: 가림이 피사체를 너무 덮지 않게(핵심은 보이게); 전경은 충분히 흐리게.

### 프레임 인 프레임 / Frame-within-a-Frame

- **시그널**: 문·창·아치·거울 등 화면 속 또 하나의 틀로 피사체를 감싸 시선을 가두고 깊이를 더하는 구도.
- **앵커**: `frame within a frame` · **의도**: focusing, layered
- **컴포지션 블록**:
  ```
  Frame-within-a-frame composition using a doorway, window, arch, mirror or gap as an inner border that encloses the subject, drawing the eye inward and adding a second layer of depth; the inner frame sits around the subject placed on a third, the darker surround concentrating attention; deep layered space through the opening; overall framing — Frame-within-a-Frame, focusing and layered.
  ```
- **영상 추가**: `slow push through the inner frame toward the subject`
- **주의**: 내부 프레임이 화면을 과하게 먹어 피사체가 작아지지 않게; 둘러싼 테두리는 다소 어둡게.

### 피겨-그라운드 분리 / Figure-Ground

- **시그널**: 피사체를 배경과 톤·색·초점으로 강하게 분리해 즉시 도드라지게 하는 구도.
- **앵커**: `strong figure-ground separation` · **의도**: clear, punchy
- **컴포지션 블록**:
  ```
  Figure-ground composition isolating the subject from its background through strong contrast in tone, color or focus, so the figure pops instantly and reads as the unmistakable focal point; subject on a third against a clean, simplified ground that recedes; clear depth separation; overall framing — Figure-Ground, clear and punchy.
  ```
- **영상 추가**: `slight parallax move reinforcing subject-background separation`
- **주의**: 배경이 피사체와 톤/색이 비슷하면 묻힘 — 대비를 만들 것(밝은 피사체엔 어두운 배경 등).

### 오버랩 깊이단서 / Overlap Depth Cues

- **시그널**: 물체들이 서로 겹쳐 가리며 앞뒤 순서를 알려 자연스러운 깊이를 만드는 구도.
- **앵커**: `overlapping depth cues` · **의도**: natural, deep
- **컴포지션 블록**:
  ```
  Overlap-based composition staggering objects so nearer ones partially cover those behind, using occlusion as the primary depth cue to build a convincing sense of front-to-back order; the subject sits within the overlapping chain on a third, each layer reading its distance; continuous receding depth; overall framing — Overlap Depth, natural and deep.
  ```
- **영상 추가**: `dolly move that shifts the overlaps and reveals depth order`
- **주의**: 겹침이 어색한 '꼬치' 배열(머리에서 나무가 자라는 식)이 되지 않게 정렬.

### 대기원근 실루엣 레이어 / Atmospheric Layers

- **시그널**: 안개·연무로 멀어질수록 옅어지는 겹겹의 실루엣 산·건물 층으로 깊은 공간을 만드는 구도.
- **앵커**: `atmospheric perspective, layered silhouettes` · **의도**: deep, ethereal
- **컴포지션 블록**:
  ```
  Atmospheric-perspective composition stacking receding layers of silhouettes — ridgelines, buildings, trees — each paler and lower in contrast than the one before as haze builds with distance, creating immense ethereal depth; the subject anchors a near layer on a third against the fading planes beyond; soft luminous recession; overall framing — Atmospheric Layers, deep and ethereal.
  ```
- **영상 추가**: `slow push or parallax revealing the receding hazy layers`
- **주의**: 층마다 대비/명도를 단계적으로 줄여야 효과 — 다 진하면 평평해짐.

### 딥티크 / 분할 프레임 / Diptych · Split Frame

- **시그널**: 화면을 둘로 갈라 두 요소를 나란히 대비시키는 구도(병치/대조).
- **앵커**: `diptych split-frame composition` · **의도**: comparative, graphic
- **컴포지션 블록**:
  ```
  Split-frame diptych composition dividing the image into two halves by a strong central edge — a wall, doorframe, horizon or color block — placing a distinct element in each side to juxtapose or contrast them; balanced weight across the divide, each half its own little scene; flat graphic depth; overall framing — Diptych Split, comparative and graphic.
  ```
- **영상 추가**: `slow move along the dividing line, or rack focus between the two halves`
- **주의**: 분할선이 명확해야 — 흐릿하면 그냥 어수선; 두 쪽이 대비/관계로 묶여야 의미.

### 룰 오브 오즈 / 홀수 / Rule of Odds

- **시그널**: 피사체를 홀수(3·5개)로 배치해 짝수보다 자연스럽고 시선이 머무는 구도.
- **앵커**: `rule of odds, odd-numbered subjects` · **의도**: natural, pleasing
- **컴포지션 블록**:
  ```
  Rule-of-odds composition arranging an odd number of subjects — three or five — so the eye settles naturally rather than splitting evenly as it would with a pair; the central or dominant one anchors a third while the others balance around it, forming a loose triangle; layered depth among the group; overall framing — Rule of Odds, natural and pleasing.
  ```
- **영상 추가**: `slow arc revealing the odd grouping in depth`
- **주의**: 홀수라도 일렬 등간격은 단조 — 무리에 강약/깊이를 줄 것.
