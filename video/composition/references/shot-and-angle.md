# 샷 사이즈 & 카메라 앵글 & 시점 (Shot Size, Camera Angle, Vantage)

프레이밍의 기본 문법 — 피사체가 화면을 얼마나 채우는지(샷 사이즈), 카메라가 위/아래 어디서 보는지(앵글), 누구의 시점인지(POV).

> 각 항목 구성: **시그널**(구도 시그니처) / **앵커**(룩을 고정하는 키워드, 있으면) / **의도**(무드 단어) / **컴포지션 블록**(영어, 바로 붙여쓰기) / **영상 추가**(영상 생성 시 카메라 무브) / **주의**.
>
> 컴포지션 블록은 이미지·영상 **공용**이며 프롬프트 **맨 앞**에 둔다. 영상은 블록을 그대로 쓰고 *영상 추가* 토큰을 덧붙인다. 사용법·삽입 순서·안 통하는 것은 `SKILL.md` 참고.



## 샷 사이즈 (프레이밍 스케일)

### 익스트림 와이드 / 설정샷 / Extreme Wide · Establishing Shot

- **시그널**: 피사체가 광활한 환경 속 작은 점처럼 놓여 장소와 스케일을 먼저 보여주는 establishing 컷.
- **앵커**: `extreme long establishing shot` · **의도**: vast, contextual
- **컴포지션 블록**:
  ```
  Extreme wide establishing shot with the subject reduced to a small element low in the frame, dwarfed by a vast surrounding environment that fills most of the image; eye-level to slightly high angle taking in the whole landscape; subject placed off-center on a third while sky or terrain dominates the negative space; deep layered space from foreground through far background with crisp deep focus; overall framing — Extreme Wide Establishing, vast and contextual.
  ```
- **영상 추가**: `slow aerial push-in or crane reveal toward the tiny subject`
- **주의**: 피사체가 너무 작아 사라지지 않게 환경과 톤 대비로 분리; 디테일 컷이 필요하면 따로 클로즈업을 잡을 것.

### 와이드샷 / 롱샷 / Wide · Long Shot

- **시그널**: 피사체를 머리부터 발끝까지 담되 주변 공간도 넉넉히 보이는 전신 환경 컷.
- **앵커**: `full-length wide shot` · **의도**: spacious, situational
- **컴포지션 블록**:
  ```
  Wide long shot framing the subject head-to-toe within a generous surrounding environment, the figure occupying a moderate portion of the frame with clear space above and around; neutral eye-level angle, subject set on a third line with the setting filling the remainder; distinct foreground, midground and background layers in deep focus; overall framing — Wide Long Shot, spacious and situational.
  ```
- **영상 추가**: `slow dolly-in or lateral tracking that keeps the full figure in frame`
- **주의**: 전신이 잘리지 않게 발밑·머리 위 여유를 둘 것; 너무 넓으면 표정·디테일이 약해짐.

### 풀샷 / Full Shot

- **시그널**: 피사체 전신이 프레임을 거의 꽉 채워 머리끝과 발끝이 가장자리에 닿는 컷.
- **앵커**: `full body shot, head-to-toe` · **의도**: complete, presentational
- **컴포지션 블록**:
  ```
  Full shot with the subject's entire body filling the frame head-to-toe, edges just clearing the head and feet; eye-level angle, figure centered or on a third with minimal surrounding space; shallow-to-moderate depth keeping the subject crisp against a soft setting; overall framing — Full Shot, complete and presentational.
  ```
- **영상 추가**: `steady tracking or slow orbit holding the full body`
- **주의**: 발끝·정수리가 어색하게 잘리지 않게 약간의 여백; 패션/제품이면 실루엣 전체가 보이게.

### 카우보이샷 / 아메리칸샷 / Cowboy · American Shot

- **시그널**: 허벅지 중간부터 위로 잡아 자세와 손, 표정을 함께 보여주는 서부극 유래 컷.
- **앵커**: `cowboy shot, mid-thigh framing` · **의도**: confident, poised
- **컴포지션 블록**:
  ```
  Cowboy shot framing the subject from mid-thigh upward, including hands and stance while staying close enough to read the face; slightly low eye-level angle for presence; figure on a third with moderate headroom; subject sharp against a softly separated background; overall framing — Cowboy Shot, confident and poised.
  ```
- **영상 추가**: `slow push-in from mid-thigh framing toward a medium shot`
- **주의**: 무릎이나 손목 관절 바로 위에서 자르지 말 것(어색함); 허벅지 중간에서 깔끔히.

### 미디엄샷 / Medium Shot

- **시그널**: 허리 위를 담아 인물과 약간의 환경, 제스처를 균형 있게 보여주는 표준 대화 컷.
- **앵커**: `medium shot, waist up` · **의도**: natural, conversational
- **컴포지션 블록**:
  ```
  Medium shot framing the subject from the waist up, balancing the figure with a readable slice of the setting; neutral eye-level angle; head positioned with comfortable headroom near the upper third, body filling the lower frame; gentle background separation with moderate depth of field; overall framing — Medium Shot, natural and conversational.
  ```
- **영상 추가**: `subtle handheld sway or slow push-in for conversation`
- **주의**: 허리에서 자르되 헤드룸 과다/부족 주의; 양손 제스처가 잘리면 답답해 보임.

### 미디엄 클로즈업 / Medium Close-Up

- **시그널**: 가슴·어깨 위를 담아 표정을 키우되 약간의 어깨선으로 인물을 안정시키는 인터뷰 컷.
- **앵커**: `medium close-up, chest up` · **의도**: engaged, intimate
- **컴포지션 블록**:
  ```
  Medium close-up framing from the chest and shoulders up, the face large enough to read every expression while the shoulders anchor the figure; eye-level angle, eyes on the upper-third line with slight headroom; soft background fall-off isolating the subject; overall framing — Medium Close-Up, engaged and intimate.
  ```
- **영상 추가**: `slow creeping push-in toward the eyes`
- **주의**: 정수리를 너무 자르거나 헤드룸이 비지 않게; 시선 방향에 약간의 룩룸을 남길 것.

### 클로즈업 / Close-Up

- **시그널**: 얼굴이 프레임을 채워 감정과 디테일을 정면으로 들이대는 강조 컷.
- **앵커**: `close-up portrait` · **의도**: emotive, focused
- **컴포지션 블록**:
  ```
  Close-up filling the frame with the subject's face from just above the head to the collarbone, every feature and micro-expression prominent; eye-level angle for direct connection, eyes near the upper third; very shallow depth of field melting the background into soft bokeh; overall framing — Close-Up, emotive and focused.
  ```
- **영상 추가**: `near-static hold with breathing micro-movement, slow focus pull`
- **주의**: 와이드 렌즈 왜곡으로 코가 커지지 않게 망원 계열로; 캐치라이트로 눈을 살릴 것.

### 익스트림 클로즈업 / Extreme Close-Up

- **시그널**: 눈·입술·손끝 같은 한 디테일만 화면 가득 채워 긴장과 질감을 극대화하는 컷.
- **앵커**: `extreme close-up, eyes only` · **의도**: tense, tactile
- **컴포지션 블록**:
  ```
  Extreme close-up isolating a single detail — the eyes, lips or fingertips — filling the entire frame so texture and tension dominate; macro-tight framing, neutral or slightly canted angle; the detail centered or on a third with everything else cropped away; razor-shallow focus on the key plane, rest dissolving; overall framing — Extreme Close-Up, tense and tactile.
  ```
- **영상 추가**: `micro slow zoom, faint focus drift across the detail`
- **주의**: 무엇을 보여줄지 단 하나로 좁힐 것; 너무 추상적이면 피사체가 안 읽힘.

### 인서트 / 디테일샷 / Insert · Detail Shot

- **시그널**: 손에 든 물건·라벨·질감 등 특정 사물 디테일만 크게 잡는 컷어웨이.
- **앵커**: `insert detail shot` · **의도**: specific, descriptive
- **컴포지션 블록**:
  ```
  Tight insert shot isolating a single object or surface detail that fills the frame, all context cropped out so the item reads instantly; slightly high or top-down angle onto the object; the key element centered or on a third, clean separation from a soft background; shallow focus on the object's face with crisp texture; overall framing — Insert Detail, specific and descriptive.
  ```
- **영상 추가**: `slow slider move across the object surface`
- **주의**: 제품 디테일이면 `product-shot` 스킬과 겹치므로 그쪽으로; 손·반사로 사물 식별을 방해하지 말 것.

### 투샷 / Two-Shot

- **시그널**: 두 인물을 한 프레임에 균형 있게 담아 관계·긴장을 보여주는 컷.
- **앵커**: `two-shot, balanced framing` · **의도**: relational, balanced
- **컴포지션 블록**:
  ```
  Two-shot framing both subjects together at medium distance, weight balanced across the frame so neither dominates; eye-level angle, the pair set on the left and right thirds with the space between them carrying the tension; moderate depth keeping both faces sharp; overall framing — Two-Shot, relational and balanced.
  ```
- **영상 추가**: `slow arc that shifts the balance between the two`
- **주의**: 한 인물이 다른 인물을 가리지 않게 깊이를 분리; 둘 다 표정이 읽히는 거리로.

### 그룹 / 앙상블샷 / Group · Ensemble Shot

- **시그널**: 여러 인물을 한 프레임에 층층이 배치해 집단과 위계를 보여주는 컷.
- **앵커**: `ensemble group shot` · **의도**: collective, staged
- **컴포지션 블록**:
  ```
  Group ensemble shot arranging multiple figures across staggered depth so each face stays visible, the cluster forming a clear triangular or layered shape; slightly elevated angle to separate the rows; the group balanced around the central axis with a focal figure on a third; deep focus keeping the whole group readable; overall framing — Ensemble Group, collective and staged.
  ```
- **영상 추가**: `slow dolly-back revealing the full group, or arc around the cluster`
- **주의**: 뒷줄 인물이 앞줄에 완전히 가리지 않게 층을 두고 키 차를 활용; 너무 많으면 초점 인물을 명확히.

### 오버더숄더 / Over-the-Shoulder (OTS)

- **시그널**: 한 인물의 어깨·뒤통수를 전경에 흐릿하게 두고 그 너머 상대를 잡는 대화 컷.
- **앵커**: `over-the-shoulder shot` · **의도**: relational, immersive
- **컴포지션 블록**:
  ```
  Over-the-shoulder shot with the near subject's shoulder and back of head anchoring the foreground out of focus on one side, framing the sharp far subject in the open two-thirds beyond; eye-level angle, the far face on a third with lead room toward the camera; layered depth from the soft foreground shoulder to the crisp subject; overall framing — Over-the-Shoulder, relational and immersive.
  ```
- **영상 추가**: `subtle handheld settle, slow push past the shoulder`
- **주의**: 전경 어깨가 프레임을 너무 먹지 않게(1/4 이하); 흐린 전경과 선명한 피사체의 심도 차를 분명히.



## 카메라 앵글 (수직 각도)

### 아이레벨 / Eye-Level

- **시그널**: 피사체 눈높이에서 보는 중립 앵글 — 가장 자연스럽고 판단을 배제한 시점.
- **앵커**: `eye-level neutral angle` · **의도**: neutral, honest
- **컴포지션 블록**:
  ```
  Eye-level framing with the camera at the subject's eye height, giving a neutral, even relationship with no power skew; horizon level and centered, subject placed on a third; natural depth with the background sitting calmly behind; overall framing — Eye-Level Neutral, neutral and honest.
  ```
- **영상 추가**: `steady level dolly or locked-off hold`
- **주의**: 평범해질 수 있으니 피사체 배치·심도로 흥미를 줄 것; 수평선은 기울지 않게.

### 로우앵글 히어로 / Low-Angle Hero

- **시그널**: 아래에서 위로 올려다봐 피사체를 크고 위압적으로 보이게 하는 영웅·권위 앵글.
- **앵커**: `low-angle hero shot` · **의도**: powerful, imposing
- **컴포지션 블록**:
  ```
  Low-angle shot looking up at the subject so the figure towers and dominates, made larger and more imposing against the sky or ceiling above; subject filling the upper frame with strong converging verticals, placed slightly off-center; foreground edge low and close for added scale; overall framing — Low-Angle Hero, powerful and imposing.
  ```
- **영상 추가**: `slow rising tilt up the towering figure, or low push-in`
- **주의**: 너무 극단이면 콧구멍·턱밑만 보여 우스워짐; 배경에 하늘/천장 등 위쪽 공간을 줄 것.

### 하이앵글 / High-Angle

- **시그널**: 위에서 내려다봐 피사체를 작고 취약하게 만드는 앵글.
- **앵커**: `high-angle looking down` · **의도**: vulnerable, diminished
- **컴포지션 블록**:
  ```
  High-angle shot looking down on the subject so the figure appears smaller and more vulnerable, surrounded by the ground that opens up below; subject set low in the frame on a third with generous space above, converging perspective pressing down; layered floor textures filling the negative space; overall framing — High-Angle, vulnerable and diminished.
  ```
- **영상 추가**: `slow crane-down or descending push toward the subject`
- **주의**: 인물 정수리만 크게 잡히지 않게 적당한 각도; 바닥이 단조로우면 패턴·소품으로 채울 것.

### 오버헤드 / 버즈아이 / 탑다운 / Overhead · Bird's-Eye · Top-Down

- **시그널**: 바로 위 90°에서 내려다봐 장면을 평면 지도처럼 추상화하는 컷(플랫레이 느낌).
- **앵커**: `top-down bird's-eye view, 90 degrees` · **의도**: graphic, omniscient
- **컴포지션 블록**:
  ```
  Directly overhead bird's-eye shot looking straight down at ninety degrees, flattening the scene into a graphic map-like pattern; subject and surrounding elements arranged as shapes on the floor plane, balanced around the center or on a grid; even deep focus across the flat field; overall framing — Top-Down Bird's-Eye, graphic and omniscient.
  ```
- **영상 추가**: `slow vertical rise pulling straight up, or gentle rotation of the flat plane`
- **주의**: 정수리·물건 윗면만 보이므로 식별 가능한 배치로; 인물 얼굴이 필요하면 이 앵글은 부적합.

### 웜즈아이 / Worm's-Eye

- **시그널**: 지면에 거의 붙은 극단적 로우앵글로 위로 치솟는 수직선과 하늘을 강조하는 컷.
- **앵커**: `worm's-eye view from the ground` · **의도**: monumental, dizzying
- **컴포지션 블록**:
  ```
  Worm's-eye view from ground level looking almost straight up, exaggerating soaring verticals that converge toward the sky or ceiling far above; subject or structure looming enormous over the low foreground, lines racing to a high vanishing point; deep focus stretching the towering scale; overall framing — Worm's-Eye, monumental and dizzying.
  ```
- **영상 추가**: `slow tilt climbing up the converging verticals`
- **주의**: 인물엔 거의 안 쓰임(턱밑·콧구멍); 건축·나무·고층 구조에 강함.

### 더치앵글 / 캔티드 / Dutch Angle · Canted Tilt

- **시그널**: 수평선을 의도적으로 기울여 불안·긴장·혼란을 만드는 앵글.
- **앵커**: `Dutch angle, tilted horizon` · **의도**: uneasy, destabilized
- **컴포지션 블록**:
  ```
  Dutch-angle framing with the horizon deliberately tilted off level so the whole frame feels unbalanced and tense; subject set against strong diagonal lines that the tilt exaggerates, placed off-center to heighten the instability; moderate depth with the canted world pressing in; overall framing — Dutch Angle, uneasy and destabilized.
  ```
- **영상 추가**: `slow roll deepening the tilt, or handheld canted drift`
- **주의**: 살짝(10~25°)이면 세련, 과하면 의도가 너무 드러나 촌스러움; 평온한 장면엔 부적합.

### 힙레벨 / Hip-Level

- **시그널**: 허리 높이에서 보는 낮은 카메라 — 약한 상승감과 함께 손·총·소품을 강조하는 컷.
- **앵커**: `hip-level camera height` · **의도**: grounded, kinetic
- **컴포지션 블록**:
  ```
  Hip-level framing with the camera lowered to the waist, looking slightly upward so the subject gains subtle presence while hands and held objects sit prominently in frame; figure on a third with the lower body and ground anchoring the base; moderate depth keeping action sharp; overall framing — Hip-Level, grounded and kinetic.
  ```
- **영상 추가**: `low tracking move following at hip height`
- **주의**: 얼굴 표정 비중이 줄므로 표정 중심 컷엔 부적합; 손동작·걸음 강조에 적합.

### 그라운드레벨 / Ground-Level

- **시그널**: 카메라를 바닥에 붙여 전경 지면이 크게 깔리고 피사체가 솟아 보이는 컷.
- **앵커**: `ground-level shot, camera on the floor` · **의도**: immersive, dramatic
- **컴포지션 블록**:
  ```
  Ground-level shot with the camera resting on the floor so the foreground surface sweeps large across the bottom of the frame and the subject rises beyond it; low looking-up angle, subject on a third above the dominant foreground plane; deep focus from the near ground texture to the standing figure; overall framing — Ground-Level, immersive and dramatic.
  ```
- **영상 추가**: `low ground-skimming dolly forward across the surface`
- **주의**: 전경 지면이 단조로우면 빈약해짐 — 디테일·반사·소품을 깔 것.

### 에어리얼 / 드론 / Aerial · Drone

- **시그널**: 높은 상공에서 비스듬히 내려다봐 풍경 속 피사체의 위치와 스케일을 보여주는 컷.
- **앵커**: `aerial drone shot, high above` · **의도**: sweeping, epic
- **컴포지션 블록**:
  ```
  Aerial drone shot from high above looking down at an oblique angle, the subject small within a sweeping landscape that unfurls below; figure or focal point on a third while terrain, roads or water form leading lines across the frame; vast layered depth in crisp focus; overall framing — Aerial Drone, sweeping and epic.
  ```
- **영상 추가**: `smooth high drone glide, slow descending reveal`
- **주의**: 피사체가 풍경에 묻히지 않게 톤/색 대비로 분리; 9:16보다 16:9·21:9에 적합.

### 3/4 앵글 / Three-Quarter Angle

- **시그널**: 정면과 측면 사이 45°에서 잡아 입체감과 자연스러운 볼륨을 주는 표준 인물 앵글.
- **앵커**: `three-quarter angle` · **의도**: dimensional, flattering
- **컴포지션 블록**:
  ```
  Three-quarter angle viewing the subject from roughly forty-five degrees between front and profile, revealing form and depth with a flattering sense of volume; eye-level height, face turned into the frame with lead room ahead of the gaze, subject on a third; gentle background separation; overall framing — Three-Quarter Angle, dimensional and flattering.
  ```
- **영상 추가**: `slow arc settling into the three-quarter view`
- **주의**: 시선이 향하는 쪽에 룩룸을 둘 것; 코끝이 뺨 윤곽을 넘으면 어색.

### 프로필 / 측면 / Profile · Side-On

- **시그널**: 정확히 옆에서 90°로 잡아 윤곽과 실루엣을 강조하는 그래픽한 컷.
- **앵커**: `profile side-on shot` · **의도**: graphic, contemplative
- **컴포지션 블록**:
  ```
  Pure profile shot viewing the subject from exactly ninety degrees to the side, emphasizing the clean outline and silhouette of the face or figure; eye-level angle, the profile set on one third with open lead room in the direction of the gaze; flat separation from a simple background; overall framing — Profile Side-On, graphic and contemplative.
  ```
- **영상 추가**: `slow lateral track holding the profile, or arc from profile to front`
- **주의**: 한쪽 눈만 보여 감정 전달이 제한적; 시선 앞 여백을 충분히.

### 정면 대칭 / Frontal Symmetry

- **시그널**: 피사체를 정면에서 중앙에 놓아 대칭과 직접성을 극대화하는 컷.
- **앵커**: `frontal symmetrical shot` · **의도**: direct, formal
- **컴포지션 블록**:
  ```
  Frontal shot facing the subject straight on and centered, maximizing symmetry and direct confrontation with the viewer; eye-level angle, the figure mirrored evenly around the central vertical axis with balanced negative space on both sides; flat, ordered depth; overall framing — Frontal Symmetry, direct and formal.
  ```
- **영상 추가**: `dead-straight slow push-in along the central axis`
- **주의**: 배경도 대칭이어야 효과가 산다 — 어긋난 배경은 대칭을 깬다; 자칫 경직돼 보일 수 있음.

### 백뷰 / 뒷모습 / Back View

- **시그널**: 피사체 뒤에서 잡아 인물이 바라보는 방향·세계를 함께 보여주는 관조적 컷.
- **앵커**: `back view, from behind the subject` · **의도**: contemplative, mysterious
- **컴포지션 블록**:
  ```
  Back view shot from behind the subject, the figure anchoring the foreground while the world they face opens up beyond; the subject placed low on a third with the gazed-at scene filling the frame ahead as the focus of attention; layered depth from the near back to the far view; overall framing — Back View, contemplative and mysterious.
  ```
- **영상 추가**: `slow follow behind the subject walking forward`
- **주의**: 인물 뒤통수가 핵심 대상을 가리지 않게 배치; 바라보는 대상이 비어 있으면 허전.

### 숄더레벨 / Shoulder-Level

- **시그널**: 어깨 높이에서 보는 약간 낮은 중립 앵글 — 살짝의 친근감과 안정감.
- **앵커**: `shoulder-level angle` · **의도**: approachable, steady
- **컴포지션 블록**:
  ```
  Shoulder-level framing with the camera just below eye height at the subject's shoulders, lending a faintly grounded, approachable feel without overt power skew; subject on a third with comfortable headroom; calm moderate depth; overall framing — Shoulder-Level, approachable and steady.
  ```
- **영상 추가**: `gentle handheld follow at shoulder height`
- **주의**: 아이레벨과 비슷하니 차이를 살리려면 살짝의 상승감을 유지.



## 시점 / POV

### 1인칭 POV / First-Person POV

- **시그널**: 피사체의 눈을 통해 보는 시점 — 손·발이 프레임에 들어오며 몰입을 만드는 컷.
- **앵커**: `first-person POV shot` · **의도**: immersive, subjective
- **컴포지션 블록**:
  ```
  First-person point-of-view framing seen through the subject's own eyes, with their hands, arms or feet entering the lower frame as they would in real sight; eye-level perspective aimed at what they look at, the object of attention centered or on a third; natural depth pulling the viewer into the action; overall framing — First-Person POV, immersive and subjective.
  ```
- **영상 추가**: `headcam-style handheld bob matching the subject's movement`
- **주의**: 손/팔 각도가 1인칭으로 자연스러운지; 너무 흔들면 멀미감.

### 오버더숄더 POV / Over-the-Shoulder POV

- **시그널**: 어깨 너머로 피사체가 보는 화면·대상을 함께 잡아 시선을 공유하는 컷.
- **앵커**: `over-the-shoulder POV` · **의도**: shared, voyeuristic
- **컴포지션 블록**:
  ```
  Over-the-shoulder point-of-view with the subject's head and shoulder soft in the near foreground, framing the screen, object or scene they are looking at sharply beyond; eye-level angle, the viewed target filling the open frame past the shoulder; layered depth from the blurred shoulder to the crisp subject of attention; overall framing — Over-the-Shoulder POV, shared and voyeuristic.
  ```
- **영상 추가**: `subtle settle behind the shoulder, slow push toward the viewed target`
- **주의**: 전경 어깨와 대상의 심도 차를 분명히; 어깨가 대상을 가리지 않게.

### 도어웨이 / 관음 프레이밍 / Doorway · Voyeur Framing

- **시그널**: 문틈·창·틈새 너머로 피사체를 엿보듯 잡아 거리감과 긴장을 주는 컷.
- **앵커**: `framed through a doorway, voyeur view` · **의도**: distant, tense
- **컴포지션 블록**:
  ```
  Voyeuristic framing peering at the subject through a doorway, window or gap, the dark surrounding edges of the opening forming a heavy frame-within-a-frame around the distant figure; eye-level hidden vantage, subject small and centered in the bright opening; deep focus from the near dark frame to the lit subject beyond; overall framing — Doorway Voyeur, distant and tense.
  ```
- **영상 추가**: `slow creep toward the opening, peeking push-in`
- **주의**: 둘러싼 어두운 프레임이 화면을 과하게 먹어 피사체가 작아지지 않게 균형.

### 리플렉션 POV / Reflection POV

- **시그널**: 거울·물·유리에 비친 상으로 피사체를 잡아 이중성·자기응시를 만드는 컷.
- **앵커**: `reflection in a mirror, POV` · **의도**: introspective, doubled
- **컴포지션 블록**:
  ```
  Reflection framing capturing the subject as seen in a mirror, window or water surface, the image doubled or recomposed within the reflective plane; the reflected figure placed on a third inside the frame-of-the-mirror, the real edge of the surface adding a second border; layered depth between the reflective surface and what it shows; overall framing — Reflection POV, introspective and doubled.
  ```
- **영상 추가**: `slow push toward the reflection, rack focus between glass and image`
- **주의**: 카메라/촬영자가 의도치 않게 반사에 비치지 않게; 반사상과 실물의 관계를 분명히.

### CCTV / 감시 코너 / Surveillance Corner

- **시그널**: 천장 모서리 높은 고정 시점에서 광각으로 내려다보는 감시카메라 컷.
- **앵커**: `surveillance camera high corner view` · **의도**: detached, clinical
- **컴포지션 블록**:
  ```
  Surveillance-style framing from a fixed high corner near the ceiling, looking down across the whole room with a wide, slightly distorted field of view; subjects small within the deep space, arranged across the floor with no favored focal point; flat deep focus and an impersonal, locked-off vantage; overall framing — Surveillance Corner, detached and clinical.
  ```
- **영상 추가**: `locked-off static hold, or stiff mechanical pan-and-tilt sweep`
- **주의**: 의도적으로 차갑고 무관심한 구도 — 드라마틱 조명/앵글과는 상충; 광각 왜곡을 약간 남길 것.

### 핸드헬드 숄더 팔로우 / Handheld Shoulder Follow

- **시그널**: 어깨에 멘 카메라가 피사체를 바짝 뒤·옆에서 따라가는 다큐·전쟁영화식 몰입 컷.
- **앵커**: `handheld shoulder follow shot` · **의도**: urgent, immersive
- **컴포지션 블록**:
  ```
  Handheld shoulder-mounted framing following the subject closely from just behind or beside, the figure on a third with reactive, slightly loose headroom as if chased by the lens; eye-to-shoulder height, energetic imperfect balance; moderate depth keeping the subject sharp amid the motion; overall framing — Handheld Shoulder Follow, urgent and immersive.
  ```
- **영상 추가**: `restless handheld follow, organic sway and micro-corrections`
- **주의**: 정적·우아한 무드와는 충돌; 흔들림은 '의도된 거침'으로, 멀미 날 만큼은 금물.
